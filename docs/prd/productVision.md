# Product Vision

The CRM is designed for small service-based businesses, especially in the education sector, such as private schools, learning centers, tutoring teams, and children’s academies.

## Product Purpose

The product creates a shared digital workspace where a business and its customers can manage communication, schedules, lessons, meetings, tasks, and important client information in one system.

The main goal is to replace fragmented communication across spreadsheets, messengers, calendars, and separate internal tools with one centralized CRM platform.

## Target Users

### Business users

- Business owners
- Administrators
- Managers
- Teachers or instructors
- Other employees

### Customers

- Students
- Parents or guardians
- Other service recipients

## Customer Workspace

Customers will have access to a dedicated portal where they can:

- view upcoming lessons, meetings, and other scheduled events;
- check event details, dates, times, and assigned employees;
- request participation in available lessons or meetings;
- send messages or questions directly to the business administrator;
- receive notifications about schedule changes or cancellations;
- access relevant personal and service-related information.

## Business Workspace

Business users will be able to:

- maintain a centralized client database;
- create and update client profiles;
- store contact details, notes, history, and other relevant information;
- create lessons, meetings, and other events;
- assign clients and employees to events;
- manage schedules and availability;
- notify clients about new events, changes, or cancellations;
- communicate with clients inside the platform;
- create tasks for employees;
- assign tasks, track progress, and monitor completion;
- review business activity through dashboards and analytics.

## Core Product Areas

The first major product areas are:

- Client Management
- Employee Management
- Calendar and Scheduling
- Lessons and Meetings
- Customer Portal
- Internal Messaging
- Notifications
- Task Management
- Dashboard and Analytics

## Main Business Value

The CRM helps small businesses:

- organize client and employee information;
- reduce manual administrative work;
- improve communication with customers;
- avoid missed lessons, meetings, and schedule conflicts;
- coordinate employee responsibilities;
- track operational activity;
- provide customers with a convenient self-service portal;
- make business decisions using centralized analytics.

## Product Positioning

The product is not only an internal CRM.

It combines:

- a CRM for business operations;
- a scheduling and event-management system;
- an employee task-management system;
- a communication platform;
- a self-service customer portal.

This shared environment allows both the business and its customers to work with the same up-to-date information while having different permissions and interfaces.
